# Application-wide execution scope

User expanded execution after the 100 candidates were generated: apply the same
investigation to every screen, modal and list; rank weaknesses; apply the winning
direction as the main design and graft compatible improvements from other ideas.

The original seven-round hierarchy tournament remains under its fixed contract.
After the champion is selected, execute a source and live-surface audit across:
- Every smart list, frequency view, custom list, folder, special list and trash.
- Analytics, calendar, import, shared list, authentication and error/empty states.
- Daily briefing/greeting, sequence/Zen modes, task rows and the full task drawer.
- List/cycle configuration and management, section/task menus and batch actions.
- Duration/diagnostic/monthly summary/weekly review/person profile dialogs.
- Account/security, integrations, AI assistant, shortcuts, search, prompts,
  confirmations, install prompt, persistence and toast feedback.

Record each surface, evidence, weakness, severity, competing proposal reference,
chosen repair or reason to retain it, source change and verification. Severity is
task impact (blocked/unusable, misleading, excessive effort/noise, cosmetic), not
an invented user-frequency percentage. Grafts must preserve the winning hierarchy
and existing behavior. Do not combine contradictory mechanisms just to retain
more ideas. Keep critical state and destructive consequences discoverable.

Verify local routes and dialogs with fixtures; do not call real integrations,
send messages, or mutate production accounts to demonstrate an interface.
Disclose any live surface unavailable without external credentials. Shared
primitive/style improvements may cover multiple surfaces; verify representative
variants and document the coverage instead of claiming each was individually
tested when it was not.
